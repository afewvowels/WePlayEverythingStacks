Version: 0.1.0
Date: 2026-11-03
  Features:
    - All inserters stack items on belts. Stack size is the smaller of the inserter's hand size and the researched belt stack size.
    - All loaders stack items on belts, including loaders from other mods.
    - Added a belt stack size selector to loaders.
    - Added belt stacking research (3 tiers, matching Space Age) when Space Age is not enabled.
    - Added startup settings for the max belt stack per entity and for loader stacking.
